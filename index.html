The basics

What is Nostr?

Nostr is a simple, open way for people to talk to each other online — posts, messages, and money — without asking a company for permission. Think of it like mail: you own your mailbox key, and any carrier can deliver for you. No carrier can lock you out of your own address.

Your keys are your identity

When you join, your phone creates a pair of keys — one public, one private. That's your account. No company issued it, no company can take it away, and it works in every Nostr app ever made.

Relays are mail carriers

Your posts travel through relays — small, independent servers anyone can run. If one carrier treats you badly, you switch carriers and keep your address, your followers, and your history. Nothing is lost.

There is no ban button

No headquarters, no CEO, no trust-and-safety department deciding who gets heard. Communities set their own rules on their own relays — and anyone can start a relay of their own.

1

Get a key

Open an app like Damus, Primal, or Amethyst. It creates your keys in seconds — save the private one somewhere safe.

2

Add the relay

Point the app at our relay (details below). Your posts now flow through infrastructure built for towns like yours.

3

Post — it's yours

Write, share, receive zaps (small Bitcoin tips). Your identity and audience belong to you, full stop.

What we're building

Three projects, one mission

Everything ships in the open, under the MIT license, designed from day one for slow connections and first-time users.

Relay Libre

Kit v0 shipped

A public relay for rural communities — plus the free, open kit that lets any town, library, co-op, or neighbor run a relay of their own on cheap hardware and a thin connection. One relay is a service; a thousand relays are a movement.

Puerta

Prototype live

The front door. A guided first run that takes a total beginner from zero to a safe key and a first post in about five minutes — plain language, big buttons, and a backup step people actually complete.

Basecamp

Lessons drafted

The free course where the climb starts. Short, practical lessons that teach keys, relays, safety, and zaps — built for workshops in libraries, schools, and community halls, and free to read online.

Why here

No signal? We bring our own.

Out here, the internet arrives weak, slow, and owned by somebody else — and it can be switched off, throttled, or taken away without asking. Ocotarra exists for the places the platforms forgot: we build the relay, the tools, and the know-how locally, so a town's voice runs on infrastructure nobody can pull out from under it.

Distance is the problem

Hundreds of miles from the nearest tech hub means no campus, no store, no meetup to fall back on. Help has to be built into the tools themselves — so we build it in.

Thin pipes, real lives

Every product is tuned for low bandwidth first: small payloads, patient sync, nothing that assumes a fiber line. A relay that sips data instead of gulping it.

Owned, not rented

A town that runs its own relay owns a piece of the internet outright. No landlord platform, no rent hikes, no eviction notices — infrastructure that stays.

The relay

One address. Yours forever.

Launching — Phase 1
wss://relay.ocotarra.org

Our public relay — free to read and post, tuned for low bandwidth, open to everyone: no invite, no approval, no fee. Add it to any Nostr app and your posts flow through infrastructure built for the places the platforms forgot.

Damus

Open Settings → Relays → tap + and paste wss://relay.ocotarra.org — then save. That's the whole step.

Primal

Open Settings → Network → Add Relay, paste wss://relay.ocotarra.org, and connect. Your feed starts flowing through it right away.

Amethyst

Open the relays menu from the top bar, tap + , paste wss://relay.ocotarra.org, and mark it for reading and writing.
